## Consequence / next step (important)

Removing MSW means the web app now depends on the real backend end to end — and the
transcription **trigger still does not exist** (handoff item #2): `complete_upload` queues the
`transcribe` job and nothing consumes it, so a real upload will sit at "Processing your video"
with the transcript panel in "Transcribing…" forever. Onboarding/persona works today (LLM live,
template fallback otherwise); upload + playback work today. To close the loop, wire the trigger
next: Storage `object finalized` → Pub/Sub → push endpoint → `run_transcribe` (it already takes
all deps as args), or a simpler interim inline run. Recommend doing that before any demo.
