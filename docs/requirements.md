# Requirements

## Clear Proposal

Submit a compelling vision for your AI solution, highlighting its positive impact and alignment with one of the problem statements in a deck/PPT converted into a PDF.

Clearly articulate the problem your solution addresses and how it will benefit the community.

Demonstrate the feasibility of your project and its potential for scalability.

## Functional Prototype

Build a working prototype using Google Cloud's AI tools & deployed on Cloud Run/GCP/Firebase.

Ensure the prototype effectively demonstrates your solution's capabilities and benefits.

## Comprehensive Documentation

Include a link to a public YouTube or Vimeo video or public Google drive link (3 minutes) showcasing your AI creation in action.

## Category Specification

Clearly identify the problem statement you are submitting for (Healthcare, Education, Sustainability, Accessibility, Social Good).
